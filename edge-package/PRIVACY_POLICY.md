# Privacy Policy for Chill Learn Extension

**Last Updated:** November 17, 2026

## Overview

Chill Learn ("the Extension") is a browser extension for Microsoft Edge that allows users to overlay interactive lessons and quizzes from Markdown files onto YouTube videos. This privacy policy explains what data the Extension collects, how it's used, and your rights regarding that data.

## Data Collection

### Data Stored Locally (On Your Device Only)

The Extension stores the following data locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`:

1. **Lesson Content**: Markdown lesson files you import (including any personal notes or content you add)
2. **Learning Progress**: Your progress through lessons (which cards you've seen, quiz answers, timestamps)
3. **Settings**: Your preferences (language, display options, keyboard shortcuts)
4. **History**: Your learning session history (scores, completion times, dates)

### Data NOT Collected

- **No personal identification information** (name, email, account credentials)
- **No browsing history** beyond YouTube video URLs where lessons are active
- **No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking**
- **No data sent to external servers** - all processing happens locally on your device
- **No AI/ML model calls** - the Extension uses only local algorithms

## Data Usage

All data is used **exclusively** for:
- Displaying your lessons and quizzes on YouTube videos
- Tracking your learning progress within lessons
- Remembering your preferences between sessions
- Showing your learning history and statistics

## Data Sharing

**We do not share, sell, or transmit any of your data to third parties.** All data remains on your device.

## Data Retention

- Data persists until you manually clear it via the Extension's settings or uninstall the Extension
- You can export your lessons and progress at any time
- Uninstalling the Extension removes all locally stored data

## Permissions Justification

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `storage` | Store lessons, progress, settings, and history locally |
| `activeTab` / `host_permissions` (YouTube) | Inject lesson overlay on YouTube video pages |
| `scripting` | Inject content scripts for lesson display |

## Your Rights

You have full control over your data:
- **Access**: View all stored data in the Extension's popup → "Xem chi tiết" (View Details)
- **Export**: Download your lessons and progress as JSON/Markdown
- **Delete**: Clear all data via Extension settings or uninstall
- **Modify**: Edit lessons, reset progress, change settings anytime

## Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you're a parent/guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance.
