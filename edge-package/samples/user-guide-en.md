# Chill Learn user guide

This lesson teaches the extension itself. Open any video and follow the cards.

<lesson time="5s" title="Welcome" size="banner" priority="1">
Welcome! This guide walks you through **Chill Learn** in about 4 minutes. Click **Next ▶** or press `Enter` to move on.
</lesson>

<lesson time="20s" title="Buttons on a card" priority="1">
The top-right corner of every card has five buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **⏱ 0:12** | countdown timer; click to pause / resume |
| **◀ ▶** | previous / next lesson |
| **–** | minimize: hide this card and wait for the next one |
| **✕** | hide all lessons |
</lesson>

<quiz priority="1">
You want to hide the current card and let the next lesson appear on schedule. Which button?
- [ ] ✕
- [x] –
- [ ] ◀
- [ ] ⏱
> **–** minimizes: it hides this card and waits for the next mark. **✕** hides everything until you turn lessons back on.
</quiz>

<lesson time="55s" title="Moving to the next card" priority="1">
Three ways to move on:

1. Click **Next ▶** (or `Enter`): the next card appears ==right away==
2. Press `]` for the next lesson, `[` for the previous one
3. Do nothing: when the timer runs out the card advances by itself

After a correct answer the question hides itself in 5 seconds.
</lesson>

<quiz type="multi" priority="1">
Select **every** way to reach the next card:
- [x] Click Next ▶
- [x] Press the ] key
- [x] Wait for the timer to run out
- [ ] Seek the video to the end
> Seeking the video does not advance cards; the other three do.
</quiz>

<lesson time="1m35s" title="Move and resize the card" priority="2">
- **Drag the card header** to move it
- **Drag the bottom-right corner** to change width / height
- **Double-click** the header or the corner to reset

Normal mode and fullscreen remember ==separate== sizes.
</lesson>

<lesson time="2m05s" title="The bar above the video" priority="1">
Click the **lesson name** on the bar above the video to open the *Lesson library*:

- switch lessons (each one keeps its own progress)
- **＋ Load file**, **📁 Load folder**, **✎ Create**
- **🗑** delete a lesson (click twice)
- view results so far, restart the lesson
</lesson>

<quiz priority="2">
How do you remove a lesson from the library?
- [ ] Click ✕ on the lesson card
- [x] Open the library and click 🗑 on that lesson twice
- [ ] Reload the YouTube page
> The first click turns the button into "Delete?"; the second click actually deletes.
</quiz>

<lesson time="2m45s" title="Settings right on the video" priority="2">
The **book** button in YouTube's control bar (or ⚙ on the top bar) opens the settings panel:

- text size, card width / height, 9-cell position
- 8 suggested themes, text color, accent color
- card style **Filled** or **Outline only**
- language: Tiếng Việt / English
</lesson>

<lesson time="3m15s" title="Create your own lesson" priority="1">
In the settings panel, the **Create lesson** tab offers three ways:

1. **Let an AI write it**: copy the standard prompt, paste it into an AI with your material
2. **Paste content**: flashcards, Q&A tables and notes are detected automatically
3. **Choose files**: `.md` `.txt` `.csv` `.tsv`

For PDFs use the *Full import page*.
</lesson>

<quiz type="input" priority="2">
The simplest flashcard file has one card per line: the term, a separator, then the meaning. Type **one** accepted separator (for example a comma).
= , | ; | - | :: | comma | semicolon | tab
> Accepted: tab, comma, semicolon, " - ", "::", ": ".
</quiz>

<lesson time="3m50s" title="Progress follows the lesson" priority="2">
Switch to another video or reload the page and the lesson ==continues where you left off==.

Finishing a lesson shows the **results summary** with a *Retry missed* button. Full history lives in the extension popup.
</lesson>

<quiz time="4m15s" priority="1">
You are mid-lesson and YouTube autoplays another video. What happens?
- [ ] The lesson starts over
- [x] The open card comes back and the lesson continues
- [ ] The lesson turns off
> Progress belongs to the lesson, not to the video.
</quiz>
